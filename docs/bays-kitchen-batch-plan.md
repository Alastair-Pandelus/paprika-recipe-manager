## 4. Cooking groups

Three groups, each one shop and one prep: the Thai trio (one paste session), the Indian trio (one ginger and seed prep) and the two tomato sauces. Massaman sits with the Thai sauces: it shares their lemongrass, ginger and coriander paste, lime leaves and lime juice, and overlaps them more (0.61 and 0.50) than the Indian curries (0.56, 0.45, 0.38). Sweet & Sour and Teriyaki stand alone: they share little with any sauce and need no spices, so they fit in any time.

### 1. Thai trio: Thai Green, Thai Red, Massaman (average overlap 0.61)

Overlap: Thai Green + Thai Red 0.71; Thai Red + Massaman 0.61; Thai Green + Massaman 0.50.

Shared by all: light coconut milk, tapioca starch, caster sugar, olive oil, lime juice, fresh ginger, lemongrass, fresh coriander, kaffir lime leaves, cumin seeds.

**Shopping and weighing list (one batch of each):**

| Water | 230 ml | Thai Green 40 ml; Thai Red 60 ml; Massaman 130 ml |

**Prep once for the group:**

- Peel and finely grate all the ginger in one go: 17 g in total (Thai Green 4 g, Thai Red 6 g, Massaman 7 g).
- Trim the lemongrass to the tender inner part and chop it all: 19 g in total (Thai Green 4 g, Thai Red 7 g, Massaman 8 g).
- Wash the coriander; chop the stalks and leaves separately (stalks go in the pastes, leaves at the end): 14 g in total (Thai Green 8 g, Thai Red 2 g, Massaman 4 g).
- Dice or slice all the green pepper: 28 g (Thai Green).
- Dice or slice all the red pepper: 72 g in total (Thai Green 22 g, Thai Red 50 g).
- Deseed and chop the red chillies: 9 g (Thai Red).
- Deseed and chop the green chilli: 4 g (Thai Green).
- Squeeze the lime juice: 2 1/2 tsp in total (Thai Green 1 1/2 tsp, Thai Red 1/2 tsp, Massaman 1/2 tsp).
- Toast the whole seeds in one dry pan, 1-2 minutes until fragrant, then grind: the cumin seeds can be toasted and ground in bulk and then measured out per sauce; toast Massaman's coriander seeds separately.
- Blitz the curry pastes one after another in the same mini chopper (Thai Green, Thai Red, Massaman), mildest first, each with 1 tbsp of its own water.
- Mix each sauce's tapioca starch with its own measured water in a small cup and label it.
- Open one tin of light coconut milk for the group (275 g needed); stir it well first.

**Cook order:** Thai Green → Thai Red → Massaman (mildest first if you use one pan).

### On their own: Sweet & Sour and Teriyaki

No shared prep: each is a 10-minute sauce with no spices or oil (Sweet & Sour: pineapple juice, peppers, cider vinegar; Teriyaki: tamari, maple syrup, ginger, rice vinegar). Make either alongside any group, or on its own; the amounts are in the table above.

## 5. General tips

- Weigh each sauce's ingredients into its own labelled bowls straight after the shared prep, so nothing gets double-counted when the sauces cook one after another.
- Cook a group on two rings, or in one pan, mildest first (the order above), wiping it between.
- Tapioca starch goes in at the end and needs only a gentle simmer; boiling hard afterwards thins it again.
- Cool each sauce, split into single portions at the weight in its recipe, label with the sauce and date, and freeze.
