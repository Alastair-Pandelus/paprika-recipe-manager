## ℹ️ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Tomato & Basil Sauce

140 g tinned chopped tomatoes  🟡 Fructose 70% (70 g per serving)
65 ml water
2 tbsp tomato purée  🟢 Fructose 23% (15 g per serving)
1 tsp balsamic vinegar
1 tsp olive oil  ♥ Sat fat 0.3g (2%)
1 1/2 tsp cornflour (optional, mixed with 1 tbsp of the water)
3 g fresh basil leaves (about 10 leaves), torn
1 tbsp chopped chives
1/2 tsp caster sugar
1/4 tsp salt
1/2 tsp dried oregano
1/2 tsp lemon juice
1 pinch ground black pepper


1. Warm the olive oil in a small pan over a medium-low heat (oregano and black pepper don't need frying, so no extra oil).

2. Add the tomatoes, tomato purée, water, balsamic vinegar, oregano, sugar and salt. Simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring now and then, until slightly thickened.

3. For a thicker sauce, stir in the cornflour mixed with water and simmer for 1 minute more (or simmer 10 minutes longer instead).

4. Off the heat, stir in the chives, basil, lemon juice and black pepper. Taste for salt.

## ℹ️ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Spicy Arrabbiata Sauce

190 g tinned chopped tomatoes  🟠 Fructose 95% (95 g per serving)
40 ml water
1 tbsp tomato purée  🟢 Fructose 12% (7.5 g per serving)
5 g sundried tomatoes (dry-packed, about 1 piece), finely chopped  🟢 Fructose 19% (2.5 g per serving)
1 tsp olive oil  ♥ Sat fat 0.3g (2%)
1 1/2 tsp cornflour (optional, mixed with 1 tbsp of the water)
1/2 tsp caster sugar
1/4 tsp salt
1/4 tsp lemon juice
1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
1 pinch dried chilli flakes
1 pinch ground black pepper


1. Warm the olive oil over a medium-low heat, add the cayenne and chilli flakes and stir for 20-30 seconds (chilli heat is fat-soluble, so this is the step that needs the oil).

2. Add the tomatoes, tomato purée, sundried tomatoes, water, sugar and salt. Simmer for 15-20 minutes until slightly thickened.

3. For a thicker sauce, stir in the cornflour mixed with water and simmer for 1 minute more.

4. Finish with the lemon juice and black pepper.

## ℹ️ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Tikka Masala Sauce

95 g tinned chopped tomatoes  🟢 Fructose 48% (48 g per serving)
80 ml water
30 g light coconut milk  🟢 Sorbitol 25% (15 g per serving)  ♥ Sat fat 1.1g (5%)
1 tbsp tomato purée  🟢 Fructose 12% (7.5 g per serving)
1 tsp olive oil  ♥ Sat fat 0.3g (2%)
2 tsp cornflour, mixed with 1 tbsp of the water
4 g fresh coriander (a small handful), chopped
1 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
1/2 tsp caster sugar
2 tsp lemon juice
1 1/2 tsp coriander seeds
2 g fresh ginger (about 1 tsp grated)
1/3 tsp salt
2/3 tsp sweet paprika
1/3 tsp ground turmeric
1 pinch dried chilli flakes
1 pinch ground black pepper


1. Dry-toast the cumin and coriander seeds in a dry pan for 1-2 minutes until fragrant, then grind.

2. Warm the olive oil over a medium-low heat. Add the ginger for 30 seconds, then the ground seeds, paprika, turmeric and chilli and stir for 30-45 seconds, adding a splash of the water if they catch.

3. Add the tomatoes, tomato purée, the rest of the water, sugar and salt. Simmer for 15 minutes.

4. Stir in the light coconut milk and the cornflour mixed with water; simmer for 1-2 minutes until thickened.

5. Finish with the lemon juice, fresh coriander and black pepper.

## ℹ️ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Korma Curry Sauce

90 g light coconut milk  🟡 Sorbitol 75% (45 g per serving)  ❣ Sat fat 3.1g (16%)
85 ml water
30 g tinned chopped tomatoes  🟢 Fructose 15% (15 g per serving)
15 g fresh ginger (about 2 1/2 tbsp grated)
2 1/2 tsp caster sugar
1 tsp olive oil  ♥ Sat fat 0.3g (2%)
1 tbsp cornflour, mixed with 2 tbsp of the water
1 1/4 tsp ground turmeric
1 tsp coriander seeds
1/2 tsp cumin seeds
1/4 tsp ground cinnamon (or cassia)
2 whole cloves
1 pinch ground black pepper
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp lemon juice
4 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed


1. Dry-toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 1-2 minutes until fragrant, then grind (this replaces the garam masala blend).

2. Warm the olive oil over a medium-low heat. Add the ginger for 1 minute, then the ground seeds and turmeric for 30 seconds, adding a splash of water if they catch.

3. Add the tomatoes, water, sugar, salt, cinnamon, cloves and cardamom (these infuse in the liquid, no frying needed). Simmer for 10 minutes, then fish out the cloves and cardamom pods.

4. Stir in the light coconut milk and the cornflour mixed with water; simmer gently for 2 minutes until creamy. Do not boil hard.

5. Finish with the lemon juice and black pepper.

## ℹ️ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Katsu Curry Sauce

110 ml water
80 g light coconut milk  🟡 Sorbitol 67% (40 g per serving)  ❣ Sat fat 2.8g (14%)
1 1/2 tbsp tamari or gluten-free soy sauce
1 tbsp caster sugar
1 tbsp cornflour, mixed with 2 tbsp of the water
1 tsp coriander seeds
3/4 tsp ground turmeric
1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds
1/4 tsp chilli powder (mild)
1/3 tsp cumin seeds
1/4 tsp fennel seeds
1/8 tsp mustard seeds
1 pinch ground black pepper
7 g fresh ginger (about 1 tbsp grated)
1 tsp olive oil  ♥ Sat fat 0.3g (2%)
2 tsp lemon juice


1. Dry-toast the coriander, fenugreek, cumin, fennel and mustard seeds for 1-2 minutes until fragrant (fenugreek turns bitter if it goes past golden), then grind. This replaces the curry powder.

2. Warm the olive oil over a medium-low heat. Add the ginger for 30 seconds, then the ground seeds, turmeric and chilli powder for 30 seconds, adding a splash of water if they catch.

3. Add the water, tamari and sugar and simmer for 5 minutes.

4. Stir in the light coconut milk and the cornflour mixed with water; simmer for 2 minutes until thick and glossy. For the classic smooth katsu sauce, blend.

5. Finish with the lemon juice and black pepper.

## ✅ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Sweet & Sour Sauce

75 ml water
75 ml unsweetened pineapple juice  🟢 Fructans 27% (38 g per serving)
2 tbsp caster sugar
15 g pineapple (fresh or tinned in juice), finely diced  🟢 Fructans 5% (7.5 g per serving)
1 tbsp tomato purée  🟢 Fructose 12% (7.5 g per serving)
1 tbsp cider vinegar
15 g green pepper, finely diced  🟢 Fructans 10% (7.5 g per serving)
12 g red pepper, finely diced  🟢 Fructose 14% (6 g per serving)
1 tbsp cornflour, mixed with 2 tbsp of the water
1 tsp chopped chives
1 pinch salt
1 pinch ground black pepper


1. Put the water, pineapple juice, sugar, tomato purée, cider vinegar and salt in a small pan and bring to a simmer. No oil is needed: there are no spices to bloom.

2. Add the peppers and pineapple and simmer for 3-4 minutes until just tender.

3. Stir in the cornflour mixed with water and simmer for 1 minute until thick and glossy.

4. Finish with the chives and black pepper.

## ℹ️ ♡ Bay's Kitchen Thai Massaman Sauce

130 ml water
90 g light coconut milk  🟡 Sorbitol 75% (45 g per serving)  ❣ Sat fat 3.1g (16%)
8 g lemongrass (about 1 stalk, tender inner part), finely chopped
7 g fresh ginger (about 1 tbsp grated)
2 tsp cornflour, mixed with 1 tbsp of the water
4 g fresh coriander, stalks and leaves
2/3 tsp coriander seeds
1/3 tsp cumin seeds
1 pinch cayenne pepper
1/4 tsp ground cinnamon
3 green cardamom pods, lightly crushed
1/8 tsp ground turmeric
1 tsp olive oil  ♥ Sat fat 0.3g (2%)
1/2 tsp salt
1/2 tsp lime juice
1/3 tsp caster sugar
2 kaffir lime leaves


1. Dry-toast the coriander and cumin seeds for 1-2 minutes, then grind.

2. Blitz or pound the lemongrass, ginger and coriander stalks with 1 tbsp of the water to a paste (no oil).

3. Warm the olive oil over a medium-low heat and fry the paste for 2 minutes, then add the ground seeds, cayenne and turmeric for 30 seconds.

4. Add the rest of the water, cinnamon, cardamom, lime leaves, salt and sugar (these infuse in the liquid). Simmer for 10 minutes; remove the cardamom pods and lime leaves.

5. Stir in the light coconut milk and the cornflour mixed with water; simmer gently for 2 minutes. Finish with the lime juice and coriander leaves.

1 tbsp chopped chives
1 1/2 tsp cornflour, mixed with 1 tbsp of the water


1. Put the tamari, water, sugar, rice vinegar and ginger in a small pan and simmer for 3-4 minutes until the sugar has dissolved. No oil is needed.

2. Stir in the cornflour mixed with water and simmer for 1 minute until glossy.

3. Stir in the chives. Use as a glaze or stir-in sauce.

Spices: whole seeds (coriander, cumin, fennel, fenugreek, mustard) are dry-toasted with no oil, then ground.
